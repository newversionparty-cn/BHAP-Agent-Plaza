# Source Verification

Use this reference when the task requires current legal, regulatory, standards, or major accident hazard basis.

## Source Hierarchy

Prefer authoritative primary sources:

1. National laws and regulations: 中国人大网、中国政府网、国务院公报、应急管理部.
2. SOE supervision: 国务院国资委 for central SOEs; local SASAC sites for province/city SOEs.
3. Safety production rules and hazard criteria: 应急管理部, 国家矿山安全监察局, 住房城乡建设部, 交通运输部, 国家能源局, 消防救援局, market supervision bodies depending on domain.
4. Standards: 国家标准全文公开系统, 国家标准信息公共服务平台, official ministry standard pages.
5. Local requirements: provincial/municipal emergency management, SASAC, housing construction, fire, transport, energy, market regulation.
6. Company basis: user-provided group制度、HSE/EHS手册、承包商管理制度、作业票制度、风险清单、隐患台账.

## Common Basis Candidates To Verify

- 《中华人民共和国安全生产法》: check the current effective text and amendment history.
- 《中央企业安全生产监督管理办法》: check the current SASAC order and effective date when central-SOE framing is used.
- 《安全生产治本攻坚三年行动方案（2024-2026年）》: check the current plan text and local implementation requirements.
- 《工贸企业重大事故隐患判定标准》: check the current emergency-management ministry order, scope, and effective date before citing.
- GB/T 33000 and related safety-production standardization standards: check the current effective edition through the official standards platform before citing.

Always re-check when the user needs final deliverables for a real current inspection, a specific region, a specific industry subtype, or a regulator-facing document.

## Citation Expectations

When sources are checked live, cite them in the final answer or document appendix. For internal working drafts, mark unverified entries as `需结合属地最新规定核验` or `待用户提供企业制度`.
